# Round-trip evaluation output

Generated by `scripts/evaluate_round_trip.py`.

- `*_hands.npz` — MediaPipe hand world/image landmarks with the paired pose, per clip (`HandClip`).
- `*_world.npz` — Pose world landmarks and visibility from the same pass, in the `extract_world_landmarks.py` format.
- `hand_scale_drift.json` — The hand round trip with a per-frame scale on these 20 clips, from `scripts/measure_hand_scale_drift.py --data-dir data/round_trip_eval`.
- `round_trip_report.json` — Body and hand round-trip errors per clip and pooled.
- `videos/` — Stitched body + hand round-trip videos per clip.
- `week2_experiments/` — Exploratory metric and smoothing experiments for Week 2 (scripts, figures, comparison videos).
- `index.md` — This index.
