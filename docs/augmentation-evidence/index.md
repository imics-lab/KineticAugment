# Augmentation evidence index

Research notes behind [`../Augmentation_Grounding.md`](../Augmentation_Grounding.md), compiled 2026-10-11, and a follow-up tolerance report (2026-11-02). Every claim carries its source URL and a verification label.

- `linguistics.md` — What varies in ASL without changing a sign (lowering, dominance, weak drop, rate, register), what is contrastive, and the ASL-LEX 2.0 fields that can gate augmentations.
- `slr_augmentation.md` — Pose augmentations used in published sign recognition (SPOTER, OpenHands, ASL Citizen, SignBERT, SAM-SLR, Spoken2Sign, …) with ranges and ablation results.
- `research_notes/` — Per-request research notes (see [`research_notes/index.md`](research_notes/index.md)), each backing a report in `reports/`.
- `reports/` — Synthesised research reports (see [`reports/index.md`](reports/index.md)), e.g. the sign augmentation tolerance report.
- `index.md` — This index.
